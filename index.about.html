Why SCID?

South Coast Industrial Door sits above our competition because of our commitment to complete client satisfaction. We offer a quality of work that is unparalleled from the rest. Here at SCID, we place a heightened emphasis on timely and on-budget service. We have implemented this requirement of quality into all aspects of our company, starting with our product and continuing down to our service.


With over 6 decades of experience in the industrial overhead door industry, we have the know-how needed to get the job done in the fastest manner. We have an experienced staff both in the office and in the field. Whatever your job requires, our staff is more than capable to handle your situation. Our field teams are equipped with the best technology and equipment available.


We are proud to say that we are Southern California's go-to overhead door company. SCID takes pride in this fact and is continuously working toward developing and improving. We go beyond your expectation in everything we do. Our driven desire for quality has proven us to be experts and a dominant force in the industry. We have gained a strong and respectable reputation throughout the area.


When doing work with us, you can expect quick responses, quality work, and a hassle-free experience. This company was founded on the core principles of honesty, integrity and family. We take pride in our work and who we are; our reputation speaks for itself. We look forward to working with you!
